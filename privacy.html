Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Effective: November 2, 2026

Push2Unlock is built privacy-first. All exercise detection happens entirely on your device, no video is ever recorded or transmitted, and the app works without any account. To improve the app we collect anonymous usage analytics that are not tied to your identity. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version: Your camera feed never leaves your iPhone. There is no sign-up, and we never collect your name, email, or contacts. We send anonymous usage analytics (which screens you use, workout results, purchase steps) to PostHog to improve the app, and purchase status to RevenueCat to unlock your subscription. We never sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

1. Who we are

Push2Unlock ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is developed and operated by Xboost. This Privacy Policy applies to the Push2Unlock iOS application and this website. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Camera & on-device processing

Push2Unlock uses your iPhone's front camera to count exercise repetitions (push-ups, squats, and sit-ups). This is the core of the app, so it is worth being precise about how it works:

  • The camera is active only while you are on the workout screen and a workout is in progress.
  • Camera frames are analyzed in real time on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework to detect body pose and joint angles.
  • No video or image is ever recorded, saved, stored, or uploaded — to us or to anyone else. Frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately.
  • No internet connection is used or required for rep counting. Detection runs fully offline.
  • We never receive, see, or have any access to your camera feed.

You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Without camera access, rep counting cannot function, but no other harm results.

3. Information we do and don't collect

We do not collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any account credentials — there is no account. The first name you type during onboarding stays on your device.
  • Camera footage, photos, or any biometric template.
  • Contacts, precise location, microphone audio, or browsing history.
  • Which apps are installed on your phone or which apps you block.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA). The app contains no ads and no advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

We do collect (anonymously)

  • Anonymous usage analytics, described in Section 5.
  • Subscription status and purchase history needed to unlock Pro, described in Section 6.

Stored on your device only

The following is created and stored locally on your iPhone to make the app work. It stays on your device (and your private iCloud, if enabled — see Section 8). These records themselves are not sent to us:

  • Your full workout history (exercise type, rep counts, dates, durations). Individual workout results are also included in anonymous analytics — see Section 5.
  • Your stats, streaks, and daily quest progress.
  • Your app settings, blocking modes, and schedules.
  • Your selection of apps to block (stored as opaque system tokens — see Section 4).

4. Screen Time & blocking data

Push2Unlock uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to block and unblock apps. When you choose which apps to block, iOS returns opaque tokens — not app names or identifiers we can read. We cannot see which specific apps you block; only the system can resolve those tokens. This selection is stored locally on your device and shared only between Push2Unlock's own app extensions on your phone. It is never transmitted to us or any third party. Analytics only ever include a count of selected apps, categories, and websites — never which ones.

5. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which features help people and where the app falls short, Push2Unlock sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (PostHog, Inc., servers in the United States). The app creates a random identifier when it is first installed and attaches it to these events. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or any account, and we do not try to identify who you are.

What is sent

  • App usage: app opens, which screens you view, onboarding steps completed, buttons such as starting a lock or ending a break early, and settings you change (for example language or iCloud sync).
  • Workout results: exercise type, target and completed reps, workout duration, the break length you chose, and whether the workout was completed or quit. Only these numbers are sent — never camera frames or body-pose data.
  • Onboarding answers: the choices you tap in the onboarding quiz — goals, estimated and ideal daily screen time, which kinds of apps distract you (picked from a list), main focus, and age range. Your typed name is never sent.
  • Subscription steps: paywall views, which plan you tried to buy, and whether the purchase succeeded, was cancelled, or failed.
  • Permission results: whether you allowed Screen Time, notifications, and camera access.
  • Device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, app language, and time zone.

Like any internet request, sending events reveals your device's IP address to PostHog. The app tells PostHog not to use it to look up your location, and we do not use it to identify you.

How it is used

Analytics are used only to fix problems and improve Push2Unlock — for example, to see where people get stuck in onboarding or which exercises they prefer. They are never used for advertising or marketing, never combined with data from other companies, and never sold or shared with data brokers. Workout results are used only to improve the app's workout and rep-counting experience. We keep analytics only as long as they are useful for improving the app.

Turning analytics off

Analytics are optional. Turn off Settings → Privacy & Legal → Share Anonymous Analytics in the app at any time; from then on no analytics events are sent. Turning it back on resumes sending. The app works exactly the same either way.

PostHog processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy and security commitments; see PostHog's Privacy Policy.

6. Subscriptions & purchases

Push2Unlock offers auto-renewing subscriptions. All payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We never receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app, your App Store purchase receipts and transaction history for Push2Unlock, and basic device and app information (such as iOS version, app version, country/storefront, and IP address). It does not receive your name, email, or payment card. This data is used only to validate purchases, restore them, and report subscription status to us. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Third-party services

Push2Unlock relies on the following, and nothing more:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — for subscriptions and purchases.
  • RevenueCat — to validate and restore subscriptions (see Section 6).
  • PostHog — for anonymous usage analytics (see Section 5).
  • Apple iCloud (optional) — to sync your own data across your devices, if you have iCloud enabled (see Section 8).

Each of these providers handles data only to provide its service to us and is required to protect it at least as well as described in this policy. We do not use any advertising, cross-app tracking, or data-broker services, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

8. Data storage & iCloud

Your workouts, stats, and settings are stored on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, this data may sync to your private iCloud account so it is available across your devices and can be restored if you reinstall the app. This data lives in your iCloud, encrypted and controlled by your Apple ID — we have no access to it. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app and removing its data from iCloud in your device settings.

9. Your rights & choices

Your workouts, stats, and settings live on your device, not on our servers. The only data held by our providers is the anonymous analytics and subscription data described in Sections 5 and 6. You remain in control:

  • Access / portability: your workout data is on your device and visible in the app's Stats screen.
  • Deletion: deleting the app removes its local data and its anonymous analytics identifier; a reinstall starts with a new, unrelated identifier. You can also clear the app's iCloud data from device settings.
  • Stop analytics: turn off "Share Anonymous Analytics" in the app's Settings (see Section 5).
  • Analytics and subscription data: email us to ask for analytics or subscription data to be deleted. Because this data is anonymous, we may need details from you (such as the approximate date you installed the app) to find it.
  • Permissions: revoke Camera, Screen Time, or notification access anytime in iOS Settings.

If you are in the EEA/UK (GDPR), we process anonymous analytics on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app, and subscription data to perform our contract with you. You may object to analytics at any time with the in-app switch, and request access to or deletion of this data by contacting us. If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA), we do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under applicable law.

10. Children's privacy

Push2Unlock is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described in Section 5 are anonymous and never include a name, email, birthdate, or precise location; onboarding only records a broad age range. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your data is protected by your device's hardware encryption and iOS sandboxing. Sensitive processing (rep detection) happens in-memory and is never persisted. Analytics and subscription data are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to PostHog and RevenueCat, which store them securely. We operate no servers of our own that store your personal data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice within the app. Continued use of Push2Unlock after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Reach us at victor@xboostapp.io and we'll respond as soon as we can.
